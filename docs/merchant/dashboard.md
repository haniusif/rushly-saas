# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your landing page — a quick read on how your deliveries and
money are doing.

## What you'll see

- **Key numbers** — totals such as shipments, and your current balance.
- **Shipment pipeline** — how many parcels are in each stage (e.g. assigned to
  courier, partially delivered, delivered).
- **Finance snapshot** — recent income vs. expense.
- **Recent shipments** — your latest parcels with their current status.

## Tips

- Click any shipment in the recent list to open its details and tracking
  timeline.
- Use the sidebar to drill into [Shipments](shipments.md),
  [Reports](reports.md), or [Finance](finance.md) for the full picture.
