# Finance

Everything about the money: what you're owed, what's moved, and the paperwork.

## Statements

Your **account statement** over a date range — a running record of COD
collected, charges, and payouts. Filter by date to reconcile a period.

## Account Transactions

The detailed **ledger**: every credit (e.g. COD collected on your behalf) and
debit (e.g. delivery/COD charges, payouts). Filter to trace a specific amount.

## Invoices

Rushly's **invoices** to you for delivery and related charges. Open an invoice to
view the breakdown and download it (PDF) for your records.

## Payment Received

Online **payments received** — e.g. prepaid/online collections tied to your
shipments, with method, transaction id, and amount.

## Reconciling

- Start with **Statements** for the period, then drill into
  **Account Transactions** for line-by-line detail.
- Cross-check **Invoices** for the charges side and **Payment Received** for
  online collections.
