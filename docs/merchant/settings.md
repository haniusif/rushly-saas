# Settings

Your rate configuration and integration settings.

## COD Charges

View the **cash-on-delivery charges** that apply to your account — the fee Rushly
takes for collecting COD on your behalf. These are set by your Rushly agreement;
this page lets you see what's currently applied.

## Delivery Charges

View your **delivery charges** — typically by weight and zone (e.g. same-day,
next-day, main city, remote area). Use these to price your own shipping to
customers.

## Also in Settings

- **[API Keys](api-keys.md)** — create keys for the Merchant API.
- **[Webhooks](webhooks.md)** — get real-time shipment event notifications.

## Account

Your profile, password, language, and theme are in the **top bar** (click your
name / the avatar), not in Settings.
