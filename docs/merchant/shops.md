# Shops

Shops are your **pickup locations** — where couriers collect parcels from. Each
shipment is picked up from one of your shops.

## Manage shops

- **Add a shop** with its name, address, and contact details.
- **Edit** or remove shops as your operation changes.
- Mark one shop as your **default** — it's pre-selected when you create a
  shipment, so most orders need no extra thought.

## Why it matters

- The default shop is what the API and quick-create flows use automatically.
- Keep addresses and phone numbers accurate so pickups aren't delayed.
