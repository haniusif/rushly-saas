# Support

Need help with a shipment, a payout, or anything else? Use the Support section
to reach the Rushly team.

## Raise a ticket

- Click **Add / New ticket**, give it a subject and description, and submit.
- Reference a tracking number when your question is about a specific shipment —
  it speeds things up.

## Track your tickets

- See all your tickets and their **status** (open, in progress, resolved).
- Open a ticket to read replies and continue the conversation.
